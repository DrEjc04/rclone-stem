Privacy policy

rclone-stem is a Google OAuth client used only by its owner, to let rclone on the owner's own computer read and write the owner's own Google Drive files.

  • Google Drive data is accessed only on the owner's computer, to copy files between Google Drive and a local folder.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
